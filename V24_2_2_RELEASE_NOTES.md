# Shop&Drop V24.2.2 — Handmade Furniture & Woodwork

- Extends **Arts, Crafts & Handmade** with dedicated handmade furniture branches for home-based makers and craftspeople.
- Adds tables/desks, chairs/benches, shelving/storage, cabinets/cupboards, bedroom furniture, outdoor/garden furniture, children’s furniture, custom/bespoke furniture, decorative woodwork and other handmade furniture.
- Keeps these as normal physical products using Shop&Drop inventory, cart, checkout, tracking and payout workflows.
- Retains the master catalogue approach so handmade products remain compatible with ordinary product search/category discovery rather than becoming an isolated marketplace.
