How to advertise your service

  1. Sign in or create your free account.
  2. Tap Advertise Your Services — FREE. Choose your service, area and rates, and add photos.
  3. Submit your advertisement. Use Requests for My Service Advertisements to manage requests.
  4. After completing a paid service, get the six-digit Completion PIN from the paying customer. Go to My Account — Receive My Payment.

How to book a service

  1. Tap Search Other Services and choose a service, rate and appointment time.
  2. Sign in and add your choice to My Service Bag. Send an appointment request.
  3. Wait for the appointment to be agreed. Open My Service Appointments, complete the required address details and follow checkout instructions.
  4. Give the six-digit Completion PIN to the person doing the work only after the agreed service is finished and you are satisfied.

Payment method

Visa, Mastercard and Instant EFT are planned for this division. Payment is not active, and no banking details or money are required.

How an Other Service booking works

Choose service → agree on date, time, location and price → pay through Shop&Drop → receive a private six-digit Completion PIN → provider completes the work → customer or authorized recipient gives PIN to provider → provider submits PIN through Shop&Drop → verified completion prepares held proceeds for automatic release, subject to provider approval and safeguards → feedback. Photographs are optional supporting evidence.

Open-ended quotation-heavy building/painting projects are outside the initial service scope.
