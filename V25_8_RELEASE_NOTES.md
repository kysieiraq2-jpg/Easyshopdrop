# Shop&Drop V25.8 Release Notes

## International marketplace foundation
- One worldwide catalogue; no duplicated country marketplaces.
- Structured country/region/city location for item physical dispatch origin, services and dispatch routes.
- Seller account country is separate from each item physical location and payout country.
- ISO listing currency selectors and country-aware defaults in preview forms.
- International phone/country selector groundwork.
- Buyer location filters: my country first, international, or all locations.
- Cross-border Dispatching & Transport collection/destination geography and route type.
- International checkout/tracking wording for courier eligibility, customs/import and verified delivery.
- Bulk/multiple-product listing groundwork while retaining one unique listing/reference per item.
- Category-specific bulk specifications remain supported by design (e.g. automotive part/OEM number and vehicle compatibility).

## Privacy and safety
- Public listings show useful country/region/city information, not private street addresses.
- Cross-border availability remains subject to courier coverage, legal/product restrictions, customs and payment-provider support.
