Shop&Drop Terms & Conditions

Shop&Drop provides an intermediary marketplace connecting independent buyers, sellers, service providers and transport providers throughout South Africa.

These Terms explain the responsibilities of participants and the services provided by Shop&Drop.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft. Subject to final legal, payment-provider and operational review.

Important: Shop&Drop facilitates transactions. It does not ordinarily purchase, own, manufacture or resell the independent goods advertised by marketplace users. The payout arrangements described below are intended launch processes and are not yet activated.

1. About Shop&Drop

Shop&Drop provides an automated intermediary service that makes buying, selling and arranging services simpler, more convenient and more cost-effective.

The platform facilitates advertising, product searches, checkout, courier arrangements, notifications, tracking and relevant transaction records.

The sale of an advertised product ordinarily takes place between the independent buyer and seller.

An independent service or transport agreement ordinarily takes place between the customer and the applicable independent provider.

Shop&Drop does not ordinarily become the seller, manufacturer, repairer or carrier of independent advertised goods.

Shop&Drop remains responsible for performing its own intermediary duties and complying with applicable law.

The initial launch is restricted to South Africa. International functionality may be introduced later, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

2. Registration and Accounts

  • Public advertisements may be browsed without registration.
  • Ordinary registration and listing are free.
  • Users must supply accurate account and contact information.
  • Users must protect login credentials and security codes.
  • Sellers and providers must satisfy applicable eligibility and verification requirements.
  • Fraudulent accounts and impersonation are prohibited.

Shop&Drop is designed to process routine account, listing and transaction operations automatically.

Its two administrators primarily handle exceptional complaints, disputes, security concerns and cases requiring human review.

Accounts may be restricted where reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, comply with law or protect marketplace participants.

3. Products and Advertisements

Independent sellers are responsible for accurately describing the goods they advertise.

Descriptions should include relevant condition, dimensions, materials, specifications, known defects and photographs.

Lawful second-hand, handmade, homemade, refurbished and pre-owned goods are permitted.

Second-hand and handmade goods may have ordinary wear, variations or imperfections. Buyers should assess the advertised description, condition and price before purchasing.

Sellers must own or be legally authorized to sell the advertised item and must supply the item agreed upon in the transaction.

Shop&Drop does not ordinarily inspect, test or independently certify each listed product.

Publication of an advertisement is not a guarantee of its quality, safety, condition or suitability.

Each listing and transaction has an identifying reference for ordering and tracking.

Unlawful, fraudulent or misleading advertisements may be restricted or removed.

4. Other Services and Dispatching

Independent providers must accurately describe their services, vehicles, equipment, pricing units, availability and operating areas.

Providers must identify their base town or location and the geographical areas they serve, where applicable.

Additional travel or equipment repositioning charges must be disclosed and agreed before payment.

Service providers are responsible for the agreed work and any qualifications, licenses or authorizations required by law.

Transport providers are responsible for their vehicles, lawful operation, capacity and applicable carriage obligations.

Customers must provide accurate booking, collection and destination information.

Simply arriving at a service location does not establish completion of cleaning, pest control or other work.

For hourly machinery, completion may be measured according to the agreed and verified paid working period.

Additional hours or additional work require an appropriate agreement between the parties.

5. Prices and Marketplace Fees

Sellers and providers enter the amount they wish to receive for their products or services.

Shop&Drop adds a buyer-paid marketplace service fee to the relevant amount.

The intended commission is:

  • 10% of the advertised amount below R1,000.
  • 7% of the advertised amount of R1,000 or more.

The applicable marketplace fee and customer-facing price must be disclosed before checkout.

Courier or transport charges are separate from Shop&Drop's marketplace commission.

Customers must be able to review the applicable total before authorizing payment.

Ordinary registration and advertising remain free.

6. Payments and Settlement

Shop&Drop intends to use an approved payment provider to facilitate secure marketplace payments and allocations.

The intended customer experience provides one coordinated checkout, even where separate underlying payment allocations are required.

These allocations may include:

  • The independent seller's product proceeds.
  • Shop&Drop's buyer-paid marketplace commission.
  • The authorized courier or transport charge.
  • The independent service or dispatch provider's proceeds.

Shop&Drop does not intend to receive the entire buyer payment into its own bank account for later manual distribution.

The seller or provider's proceeds are intended to remain under the authorized payment provider's applicable holding and settlement controls until the relevant verified payout milestone.

For ordinary physical goods, the intended payout milestone is verified courier collection.

For Other Services, the intended payout milestone is verified completion of the agreed service.

For Dispatching, the milestone is verified completion of the agreed transport service or paid equipment-working period.

Payout eligibility does not guarantee immediate arrival of money in a bank account.

Actual settlement depends on provider capabilities, applicable banking rules and lawful financial safeguards.

The final payment arrangements remain subject to provider approval, legal review and successful testing before live payments are enabled.

7. Orders and Inventory

For eligible quantity-limited goods, Shop&Drop temporarily reserves available stock during checkout.

The intended reservation period is 30 minutes.

A successful, verified payment commits the sale. Failed, cancelled or expired reservations are reconciled and released under the marketplace's inventory safeguards.

Placing an item in a shopping bag does not mean it has been sold or paid for.

Sellers should prepare goods for collection only after receiving a confirmed sale notification.

8. Courier Collection and Seller Payment

For ordinary physical-product transactions, the intended seller payout milestone is independently verified collection by the authorized courier.

The intended collection process may include:

  • Courier-held collection PIN: Shop&Drop issues a six-digit code securely to the authorized courier after confirmed buyer payment, courier payment and booking. The courier gives it to the seller or authorized handover person only after taking the parcel, and the seller enters it securely in Shop&Drop.
  • A courier scan or authorized collection confirmation.
  • The matching shipment, booking and waybill references.
  • An authenticated collection record from the authorized courier.

SELLERS MUST ASK THE COURIER FOR THE SIX-DIGIT COLLECTION PIN AT PICKUP. Submit the code in My Account only after the parcel is physically handed over, and keep the collection receipt. Authenticated courier tracking, pickup scanning or the courier-held PIN are alternative verification methods; one accepted method is sufficient, subject to security checks.

The seller's own declaration that a parcel was collected is not sufficient by itself to authorize payment.

The collection PIN is separate from the buyer's later delivery-confirmation PIN.

After confirmed payment and satisfactory collection verification, the seller's proceeds are intended to become eligible for automatic release without a routine wait for final delivery.

Applicable fraud, dispute, financial, provider and legal safeguards still apply.

Courier collection establishes evidence of physical custody of a parcel. It does not independently certify the parcel's contents or condition.

The buyer will continue to receive available shipment-tracking information.

Courier loss, damage and delivery claims are governed by applicable carriage terms, available insurance and law.

9. Completion of Other Services

Cleaning, pest control, repairs and other independent services must reach their agreed completion milestone before provider payout becomes eligible.

The service provider may report completion using authenticated evidence linked to the correct booking.

Relevant evidence may include completion photographs, time and location information, or an appropriate customer completion PIN.

A customer PIN may serve as an alternative or additional confirmation when the customer is present.

Photographs or a provider's declaration are not automatically conclusive proof of satisfactory work.

Shop&Drop intends to use automated verification and appropriate safeguards to determine whether completion evidence is sufficient.

A customer who does not respond must not automatically be allowed to delay an otherwise verified provider payment indefinitely.

Genuine disputes and mandatory financial holds may require exceptional review or other lawful action.

10. Dispatch and Equipment Completion

For ordinary dispatch or transportation, the intended payment milestone is verified completion of the agreed journey and destination handover.

Supporting evidence may include authorized GPS information, transport records, shipment references and recipient confirmation.

GPS arrival at a destination does not necessarily establish that a required handover has occurred.

For hourly cranes, bulldozers and other equipment, the relevant milestone may be completion of each agreed paid working period.

Additional work beyond the purchased period requires a further agreement where applicable.

Providers remain responsible for their own work, vehicles, equipment and applicable legal duties.

11. Returns, Refunds and Disputes

The buyer and independent seller ordinarily remain the parties to the product sale.

Disagreements about descriptions, condition, defects or workmanship ordinarily involve the buyer and seller and their respective legal obligations.

Shop&Drop facilitates exceptional complaint reporting, transaction records and communication where appropriate.

Shop&Drop does not automatically become the seller or manufacturer merely because a sale was arranged through the platform.

Courier loss or damage may involve the authorized carrier's contractual and legal obligations.

Insurance and reimbursement depend on the actual courier agreement, coverage, exclusions and applicable law.

A seller's payout following verified collection does not eliminate legally enforceable buyer rights.

Routine qualifying refunds are intended to be processed automatically where supported by the authorized payment provider.

Exceptional disputes may be considered by one of Shop&Drop's two administrators.

Any refund of Shop&Drop's marketplace commission will depend on the circumstances, provider charges and mandatory legal requirements.

Please read the Returns & Refund Policy .

12. Privacy and Communication

Public listings must not display private buyer or seller phone numbers, banking details or residential addresses.

Shop&Drop may provide authorized couriers with the necessary pickup and delivery details for their assigned transactions.

Collection PINs, service completion photographs, GPS evidence and sensitive transaction information must be handled securely.

Where a genuine dispute requires direct communication, authorized administrators may facilitate contact or disclose limited details when lawful and necessary.

Such disclosure must be proportionate and subject to the Privacy Policy and applicable data protection law.

13. Prohibited Conduct

Users must not:

  • Advertise stolen, counterfeit or prohibited goods.
  • Deliberately misrepresent a product or service.
  • Use fraudulent identities or payment information.
  • Falsely report collection, delivery, arrival or completion.
  • Misuse collection, delivery or completion PINs.
  • Submit fabricated photographs, scans or tracking records.
  • Attempt unauthorized payment or payout manipulation.
  • Misuse another user's private information.
  • Harass, threaten or defraud another participant.
  • Bypass authorized payment and fraud-prevention safeguards.

Shop&Drop may restrict, suspend or investigate unlawful or fraudulent conduct in accordance with applicable law.

14. Responsibility and Consumer Rights

Independent sellers remain responsible for the products they advertise and supply.

Independent service and transport providers remain responsible for their own services and applicable obligations.

Shop&Drop remains responsible for its own obligations as a marketplace operator and intermediary.

Applicable South African legislation may include the Consumer Protection Act, Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, POPIA and other laws.

The application of particular legal rights depends on the transaction, parties and statutory exceptions.

Nothing in these Terms excludes a responsibility or legal remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Customers and providers are encouraged to read the policies before entering transactions.

Failure to read a policy does not automatically remove mandatory statutory rights.

Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
