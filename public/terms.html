Shop&Drop Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Shop&Drop, an online marketplace connecting buyers, sellers, service providers and transport providers.

These Terms explain the responsibilities of people using Shop&Drop and the rules governing transactions through the marketplace.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft, subject to final legal and payment-provider review.

Important: Shop&Drop is currently preparing its South African marketplace launch. Live payment, settlement and courier-provider arrangements will be activated only after the required integrations and checks are completed.

1. About Shop&Drop

Shop&Drop provides a marketplace where individuals and businesses can advertise products, offer services and arrange eligible dispatch and transport services.

Shop&Drop facilitates transactions between customers and independent sellers or providers. Unless expressly stated otherwise, Shop&Drop is not the owner, manufacturer or direct supplier of items or independent services advertised by users.

The initial marketplace launch is restricted to South Africa. International features may be introduced later, subject to applicable requirements.

2. Registration and Accounts

  • Browsing public advertisements does not require registration.
  • Registration and listing are free.
  • Users must provide accurate account and contact information.
  • Users are responsible for protecting their login credentials.
  • Seller and provider approval may be required before listings become public.
  • Users must not impersonate another person or create fraudulent accounts.

Shop&Drop may restrict accounts where reasonably necessary to address fraud, unlawful activity, security risks or breaches of these Terms, subject to applicable law.

3. Products and Advertisements

Sellers are responsible for describing their products accurately, including condition, quantity, specifications, defects and relevant photographs.

New, handmade, second-hand, pre-owned and refurbished products must be described truthfully. Known defects or limitations must not be concealed.

Each product listing is associated with a Shop&Drop reference to assist with ordering, tracking and transaction records.

Shop&Drop may reject or remove misleading, prohibited or unlawful advertisements.

4. Services and Transport

Independent service providers are responsible for accurately describing the work they offer, their pricing units, availability and any qualifications or authorisations required by law.

Dispatch and transport providers are responsible for supplying accurate vehicle, capacity, availability and service information.

Customers must provide correct collection, delivery and booking information where required.

The applicable service completion or delivery verification process will depend on the type of transaction.

5. Prices and Marketplace Fees

Sellers and providers enter the amount they wish to receive for their products or services. Shop&Drop calculates the applicable marketplace service fee and displays the customer-facing price before checkout.

The planned buyer-paid marketplace fee is:

  • 10% for item or service amounts below R1,000.
  • 7% for item or service amounts of R1,000 or more.

The applicable fee and total payable must be disclosed before the customer authorises payment. Any future changes to fee rules will be reflected in the published pricing information.

Courier or transport charges, where applicable, are separate from the marketplace service fee and must be disclosed before payment.

6. Payments and Settlement

Shop&Drop is designed to use an authorised payment provider to process marketplace transactions.

The intended checkout arrangement allows the customer to make one complete checkout payment, with the relevant amounts allocated through the payment-provider arrangement.

These allocations may include the seller or service provider's proceeds, the Shop&Drop marketplace service fee and the applicable courier charge.

Shop&Drop does not intend to receive the entire transaction amount into its own bank account and then manually distribute the seller and courier proceeds.

Seller proceeds are intended to remain under the authorised provider's settlement controls until the applicable verified completion or delivery requirements are satisfied.

The final payment and settlement arrangements remain subject to banking, provider, legal and operational approval.

7. Orders and Inventory

For eligible quantity-limited products, Shop&Drop may temporarily reserve stock during checkout.

The planned reservation period is 30 minutes. A successful, verified payment commits the sale. Failed, cancelled or expired reservations are released according to the marketplace's reconciliation safeguards.

An order is not considered paid merely because a customer places an item in a bag or begins checkout. Payment must be confirmed through the authorised payment process.

8. Delivery and Completion Verification

Shop&Drop uses transaction references, courier or provider records and appropriate delivery or completion verification measures.

These measures may include tracking records, proof of delivery, a customer confirmation PIN or an authorised scanner-based confirmation.

Seller or provider payout eligibility depends on the applicable verified completion conditions and the payment provider's requirements.

Customers must not share a delivery or arrival confirmation PIN before the required handover or service milestone has actually occurred.

9. Returns, Refunds and Disputes

Customers may report items not received, damaged items, incorrect products, material misrepresentation or other eligible transaction problems through Shop&Drop.

Shop&Drop will assess requests under its published Returns & Refund Policy, the applicable transaction terms and mandatory South African consumer law.

Where appropriate, a qualifying dispute may temporarily prevent seller payout while the matter is assessed.

Refunds, including any marketplace fee adjustment or courier-related amount, depend on the circumstances, applicable law and the authorised payment-provider process.

Nothing in these Terms removes rights or remedies that customers are entitled to under applicable law.

10. Privacy and Communication

Shop&Drop does not publicly display private seller contact details, banking information or private residential addresses on marketplace advertisements.

Necessary delivery and contact information may be supplied securely to an authorised courier or provider when required to complete an approved transaction.

Personal information is handled according to the Shop&Drop Privacy Policy and applicable South African data protection law.

11. Prohibited Conduct

Users must not:

  • Advertise unlawful or prohibited products or services.
  • Provide deliberately false or misleading information.
  • Use stolen identities or payment information.
  • Attempt fraudulent orders, refunds or delivery confirmations.
  • Use Shop&Drop to arrange prohibited off-platform payments or bypass its transaction safeguards.
  • Harass, threaten or unlawfully disclose another user's private information.

12. Responsibility and Consumer Rights

Sellers and providers remain responsible for their own products, representations, services and legal obligations.

Shop&Drop is responsible for its own applicable duties as a marketplace operator.

Any limitation of liability must be interpreted subject to applicable South African law. Nothing here excludes liability or statutory rights where exclusion is not legally permitted.

13. Changes to These Terms

Shop&Drop may update these Terms as the marketplace develops, subject to applicable notice and legal requirements.

Material changes affecting users will be communicated where required.

The final approved Terms and effective date will be published before the marketplace begins accepting live transactions.
