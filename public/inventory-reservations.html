Reservation Protection Rules

Reservation hold: Shop&Drop reserves available stock for 30 minutes while payment is pending.

Successful payment: An authoritative payment confirmation commits the reservation.

Failed or expired payment: The backend releases the reservation according to its existing safeguards.

This administrator page does not control reservation processing. Late payments, duplicate payment notifications and inventory reconciliation remain handled by the protected backend.
