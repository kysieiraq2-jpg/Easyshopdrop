How to sell an item

  1. Sign in or create your free account.
  2. Tap Sell an Item — FREE. Add photos, describe the item and enter the price you want to receive.
  3. Check your details and post your advertisement.
  4. After a sale, get the courier reference when the courier collects your item. Go to My Account — Receive My Payment.

FOR PAYMENT PURPOSES

New, Second-Hand & Handmade Products: Obtain the courier company’s own booking, waybill or tracking reference when the courier physically collects your sold item. It is your responsibility to obtain this reference number for payment purposes.

No reference number or PIN is needed to register or advertise. After pickup or completion, sign in and use Receive My Payment in My Account.

Shop&Drop handles listing automatically. Payment, courier booking and payout take place separately when an item is purchased. Shop&Drop never asks for your card PIN or OTP.
