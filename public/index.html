How Shop&Drop works

1. List for FREE — sell a product or offer a clearly priced service.

2. Customer buys or books — checkout, payment, notifications and tracking stay inside Shop&Drop.

3. Complete the transaction — seller/provider fulfils it, Shop&Drop applies the clearly disclosed marketplace service fee, and payout follows the completion rules.

Sell locally. Sell internationally. Shop simply.

Shop&Drop is designed for ordinary people as well as businesses. Direct contact and sensitive payout details stay private. Sellers see their target proceeds, Shop&Drop marketplace service fee and final customer price before a listing goes live. Fee rules remain configurable and transparent.
