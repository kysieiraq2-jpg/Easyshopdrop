How to buy an item

  1. Browse or search for the item you want.
  2. Open the item, check its details and sign in when ready to add it to your Shopping Bag.
  3. Open your Shopping Bag, check your items and enter your delivery address. Follow the checkout instructions.
  4. After ordering, use My Purchases or Track My Order to check progress.
