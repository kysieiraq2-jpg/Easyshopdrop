Evidence Requirements

Dispute evidence may include buyer item photographs, original listing photographs, courier tracking records, delivery confirmation, return photographs and other relevant material.

Evidence must remain linked to the correct transaction or dispute and must be available only to authorised parties.

Production evidence uploads require protected Supabase Storage permissions, validated file types, timestamps and audit history.
