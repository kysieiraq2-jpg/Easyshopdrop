Shop&Drop Privacy Policy

Shop&Drop respects the privacy of buyers, sellers, service providers, transport providers and other people using the marketplace.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information may be collected, why it is needed, how it is used and when it may be shared.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft. Subject to final administrator and legal review.

Our privacy principle: Private seller and buyer contact information, residential addresses, banking details and transaction information must not be publicly displayed on marketplace advertisements.

1. Applicable Privacy Law

Shop&Drop intends to process personal information in accordance with applicable South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

This policy applies to personal information processed through Shop&Drop's South African marketplace, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Shop&Drop, personal information may include:

  • Name and account identification details.
  • Email address and cellphone number.
  • Login and authentication information.
  • Seller or provider registration details.
  • Private collection and delivery addresses.
  • Location and GPS information supplied for dispatch or delivery.
  • Product and service listing information.
  • Order, payment, refund and transaction references.
  • Delivery tracking and confirmation records.
  • Complaints, disputes, feedback and supporting evidence.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate the platform.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary for the relevant service, transaction, legal requirement or security purpose.

3. Why Information Is Used

Personal information may be processed to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify identities and authorised access.
  • Publish and manage eligible advertisements.
  • Process orders and service bookings.
  • Arrange authorised collection and delivery.
  • Send transaction and account notifications.
  • Support payment, settlement and refund processes.
  • Investigate complaints, disputes and suspected fraud.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Public and Private Information

Public advertisements may display product or service descriptions, photographs, prices, general locations and an approved public seller or provider name.

Private account information, residential addresses, personal contact details, banking information and sensitive transaction records are not intended for public listing display.

Shop&Drop uses internal references to associate advertisements and transactions with the appropriate accounts without unnecessarily revealing private identities.

5. Sharing Information With Providers

Information may be shared with authorised third-party providers when reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace.

These may include:

  • Payment and banking service providers.
  • Authorised couriers and transport providers.
  • Hosting, database and authentication providers.
  • Email, WhatsApp Business or notification service providers.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where legally required.

Couriers and transport providers should receive only the relevant collection, delivery and contact information necessary for their authorised assignments.

Shop&Drop does not intend to publish private seller or buyer contact information as part of public marketplace advertisements.

6. Payments and Banking Information

Shop&Drop's intended payment arrangement uses an authorised payment provider to process and allocate transaction funds.

Payment providers may require additional identity, banking or verification information to meet legal, fraud-prevention and settlement requirements.

Shop&Drop should not unnecessarily collect or store complete payment card details where an authorised payment provider can process them securely.

Final payment-provider arrangements remain subject to approval and integration before live financial transactions are enabled.

7. Account and Transaction Security

Shop&Drop uses account authentication, access restrictions and transaction controls designed to protect private information.

Access to sensitive information should be limited to authorised persons and services with a legitimate operational need.

Delivery confirmation PINs, authentication credentials and other security information must not be shared with unauthorised persons.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Suspected security incidents will be investigated and handled according to applicable legal requirements.

8. Communications and Notifications

Shop&Drop may send necessary account, transaction, delivery, payment and security notifications through supported channels.

These may include email, in-platform notifications and, where configured, WhatsApp Business or SMS.

Marketing communications will be handled subject to applicable consent and direct-marketing requirements.

Users should be able to update relevant contact information and communication preferences where supported.

9. Information Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required or permitted by applicable law.

Relevant transaction, financial and dispute records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted, de-identified or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request information about the personal information Shop&Drop holds about them.

They may also request correction of inaccurate information, object to certain processing, or request deletion where legally appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained despite a deletion request where legal obligations or legitimate transaction requirements apply.

Privacy requests should be directed to the official Shop&Drop privacy contact once published.

11. Cookies and Technical Information

Shop&Drop may use browser storage, session information and necessary technical data to support authentication, security, shopping activity and website functionality.

Any optional analytics or marketing technologies must be assessed and disclosed appropriately before activation.

Users may be able to manage certain browser storage settings through their browser or device.

12. International Data Processing

Shop&Drop is initially launching for South African marketplace transactions.

Some technology providers may process or store information outside South Africa. Any international transfer of personal information must comply with applicable POPIA requirements.

International marketplace functionality may be introduced later, subject to legal, payment and operational readiness.

13. Complaints and Privacy Enquiries

Users may raise privacy questions, request corrections or report concerns through Shop&Drop's official administration contact channels.

Where appropriate, individuals may also contact South Africa's Information Regulator regarding their rights under POPIA.

Official Shop&Drop privacy contact details will be added before public launch.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Shop&Drop's services, technology and legal obligations develop.

The final approved policy and effective date will be published before live marketplace operations begin.
