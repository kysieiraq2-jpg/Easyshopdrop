Shop&Drop Privacy Policy

Shop&Drop provides an intermediary marketplace connecting independent buyers, sellers, service providers and transport providers.

This policy explains how personal information is collected, used, protected and shared when facilitating advertisements, payments, courier collection, deliveries and services.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft. Subject to final South African legal review, official contact details and completion of payment and courier integrations.

Our privacy principle: Public advertisements may show an approved seller alias and general operating location, but not private residential addresses, personal contact numbers, banking details or confidential transaction evidence.

1. Applicable Privacy Law

Shop&Drop intends to process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), and other applicable South African legislation.

Operating as a marketplace intermediary does not remove Shop&Drop's own privacy, security, transparency and recordkeeping obligations.

Authorized payment providers, couriers and other independent service providers may also have separate legal obligations regarding information they process.

The final published policy must identify Shop&Drop's responsible party and official privacy contact details.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how a person uses Shop&Drop, personal information may include:

  • Account names and identification information.
  • Email addresses and cellphone numbers.
  • Account authentication and security records.
  • Seller and service-provider registration information.
  • Identity and bank-account verification results.
  • Private pickup, service and delivery addresses.
  • General advertised operating areas.
  • GPS coordinates necessary for dispatch or delivery verification.
  • Product descriptions, listing references and order information.
  • Payment, settlement, refund and transaction references.
  • Courier bookings, waybills, scans and tracking information.
  • Courier collection and handover records.
  • Collection, delivery and completion PIN verification records.
  • Service-completion photographs and other relevant evidence.
  • Agreed hourly equipment or machinery work-period records.
  • Complaints, disputes and supporting evidence.
  • Administrator investigation and decision records.
  • Necessary browser, device and security information.

Shop&Drop aims to collect only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant transaction, security purpose or legal obligation.

Users must not include unnecessary identity documents, personal contact information, bank details or third-party private information in public advertisements.

3. Why Information Is Used

Personal information may be used to:

  • Register and authenticate user accounts.
  • Verify sellers and service providers.
  • Publish and manage eligible advertisements.
  • Facilitate purchases between independent buyers and sellers.
  • Prepare orders and bookings.
  • Arrange authorized courier collection and delivery.
  • Coordinate dispatch, transport and equipment bookings.
  • Confirm courier collection and relevant service-completion milestones.
  • Enable authorized payment allocations and settlement processes.
  • Send transaction and security notifications.
  • Detect fraud and false completion claims.
  • Investigate exceptional complaints and disputes.
  • Comply with legal, financial and recordkeeping requirements.

Evidence obtained for a transaction must not automatically be used for unrelated marketing or public display.

4. Public and Private Information

Public advertisements may display approved photographs, item descriptions, prices, general locations, approved seller aliases and provider operating areas.

A service or transport advertisement may identify the town, province or declared operating region of a provider or vehicle.

Private residential addresses, personal telephone numbers, banking information, identity documents and confidential transaction records are not intended for public listing display.

Shop&Drop uses internal user, listing, booking, shipment and transaction references to associate the appropriate parties without unnecessarily revealing their private identities.

A buyer and seller may have different rights to access information relating to a transaction. Those rights do not automatically permit unrestricted access to each other's private records.

5. Sharing Information With Providers

Relevant information may be shared with authorized third parties when reasonably necessary to provide Shop&Drop's intermediary services.

These may include:

  • Authorized payment and banking providers.
  • Couriers and transport providers assigned to a transaction.
  • Database, hosting and authentication providers.
  • Approved email, SMS or WhatsApp messaging providers.
  • Professional advisers and legally authorized authorities.

A courier may receive the collection and delivery details required to complete its authorized assignment.

A payment provider may receive transaction references, verification results and necessary financial information to process an approved payment, settlement or refund.

Shop&Drop does not publicly disclose buyer and seller personal contact details merely because they entered into a transaction.

6. Exceptional Disputes and Contact Disclosure

Shop&Drop normally facilitates communication through its own transaction and support systems.

When a genuine dispute arises between an independent buyer and seller, Shop&Drop's authorized administrators may investigate the transaction.

Where there is an appropriate lawful basis, an administrator may facilitate direct communication or disclose limited contact information necessary to address that particular dispute.

Such disclosure must be relevant, proportionate and restricted to the authorized purpose.

Being involved in a dispute does not automatically entitle a person to another user's full account, identity, financial or address details.

Relevant administrator access and disclosure decisions should be recorded for security and accountability.

7. Courier Collection References and Security

Shop&Drop's intended physical-product process includes verified collection of the parcel by the authorized courier.

Collection verification may involve:

  • The actual courier company booking, tracking, waybill or collection reference, which can vary in length and format.
  • A courier scan or equivalent collection record.
  • The authorized courier booking reference.
  • The shipment tracking or waybill reference.
  • An authenticated courier collection notification.

The courier reference is connected with the authorized booking and shipment and may be used throughout tracking and delivery. Any buyer delivery-confirmation PIN remains separate from the courier reference.

Courier references, collection receipts, tracking events and any separate delivery codes are transaction information. Access should be restricted to authorized participants for legitimate fulfilment, security, dispute or financial purposes.

The collection record may be used to determine eligibility for seller payment under the approved payout rules, subject to safeguards.

Verification of courier collection does not independently establish the condition or contents of the parcel.

8. Other Services Completion Evidence

After confirmed payment, the customer may access a six-digit completion PIN privately and may give it to an authorized representative. The assigned provider submits it after the agreed service has been completed. Completion photographs remain optional supporting evidence and must not reveal unnecessary personal information. PINs must never be published in advertisements.

For cleaning, pest control, repairs and other physical services, Shop&Drop's intended payment process requires verified completion of the agreed service.

Relevant evidence may include authenticated completion photographs, booking information, time records, appropriate location evidence or a customer-supplied completion PIN.

The purpose of this evidence is to support verification of the service milestone and appropriate provider payment processing.

A completion photograph must not automatically be treated as conclusive proof of satisfactory work. Additional verification or exceptional review may be needed in appropriate cases.

Photographs of private residences, children, workers, confidential documents or other identifiable people must not be collected or published unnecessarily.

Where photography is inappropriate or unlawful, an approved alternative form of completion evidence should be used.

Customer-supplied completion codes must be protected against misuse. The absence of a customer response should not automatically result in an indefinite payment hold.

9. Dispatch and Equipment Evidence

The paying customer may share a six-digit dispatch completion PIN with an authorized receiver at the destination. The provider must obtain and submit the code only after the required delivery or handover is finished. GPS and transport records are optional supporting evidence rather than routine additional payout conditions. PIN verification records must be protected.

Dispatch and transport completion may be supported by authorized GPS information, destination records, delivery or handover confirmation and relevant transaction references.

For hourly machinery and equipment hire, time records may be used to verify the agreed paid working period.

GPS information must be collected and used for a legitimate, disclosed purpose connected with the relevant transaction.

Shop&Drop does not intend to make a provider's continuous private location history publicly available merely because that provider advertises a transport or equipment service.

Sensitive location information should be accessible only to authorized parties with a legitimate need.

10. Payments and Banking Information

Shop&Drop's intended payment arrangement uses an authorized payment provider to process and allocate transaction funds.

Shop&Drop intends to receive its applicable buyer-paid marketplace commission while seller, courier and service-provider proceeds are handled through approved provider arrangements.

Payment providers may require identity verification, banking information and transaction records for lawful settlement, fraud prevention or compliance.

Shop&Drop does not intend to store complete payment-card details when an authorized provider can process them securely.

The proposed payout milestones are verified courier collection for physical goods and verified completion for services and dispatch.

These payout mechanisms are not yet active. Their exact integration and information-sharing arrangements remain subject to provider approval and testing before launch.

11. Communications and Notifications

Shop&Drop may send account, order, collection, delivery, completion, payment, dispute and security notifications.

Supported communication channels may include the website, email and appropriately configured SMS or WhatsApp services.

Transaction notifications should not unnecessarily reveal another user's confidential information.

Marketing communications will be managed in accordance with applicable consent and direct-marketing requirements.

Users should keep their relevant account contact details accurate to receive important transaction and security information.

12. Information Retention and Security

Shop&Drop intends to retain personal information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the relevant legitimate purpose, or as required or permitted by law.

Transaction, financial, dispute, courier and intermediary records may require retention to satisfy applicable legal and accounting requirements.

Collection verification records, completion photographs, GPS data and PIN-related evidence should be subject to appropriate access restrictions and retention controls.

No fixed automatic deletion period is promised until the required retention schedules have been implemented and reviewed.

When information is no longer needed, it should be securely deleted, de-identified or managed in accordance with applicable law.

No online platform can guarantee absolute security. Suspected incidents will be investigated and handled according to applicable legal requirements.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access to personal information held about them, correction of inaccurate information and other appropriate privacy rights or remedies.

Certain records may need to be retained despite an account closure or deletion request where a lawful obligation or legitimate transaction purpose requires their retention.

A request for personal information does not automatically authorize disclosure of another user's private account information.

The final published policy must provide the official Shop&Drop privacy contact and request procedure.

14. Cookies and International Processing

Shop&Drop may use necessary browser storage, authenticated sessions and technical information for shopping, security and website functionality.

Optional marketing or analytics technologies must be assessed and appropriately disclosed before activation.

Shop&Drop initially launches for South African transactions. Its international capabilities are retained for possible future expansion.

Some hosting, payment or communication providers may process information outside South Africa. Any such transfers must comply with the applicable POPIA requirements and safeguards.

15. Complaints, Policy Changes and Contact

Privacy questions, access requests and complaints may be directed to Shop&Drop's official administration or privacy contact channels.

Individuals may also approach South Africa's Information Regulator where appropriate.

The official privacy contact, responsible-party details and effective date must be finalized before public launch.

This policy may be updated as legislation, approved providers and marketplace operations develop.

The final legally reviewed version will be published before live marketplace transactions begin.
