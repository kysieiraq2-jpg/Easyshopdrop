Other Service categories

Select a category to view its subcategories and browse available advertisements. Transport and dispatch jobs have their own dedicated workflow under Dispatching & Transport.

How an Other Service booking works

Choose service → select date/time and units → see the calculated amount → book/pay through Shop&Drop → provider notified → service performed → customer can confirm completion or report a problem → verified arrival → provider payout eligible (subject to payment and safety checks) → provider paid automatically → feedback.

Open-ended quotation-heavy building/painting projects are outside the initial service scope.
