Other Services

Find and book independent service providers who have listed their services on Shop&Drop. Book defined services by hour, day, kilometre, gig/event, job or treatment. Service providers choose an approved category, describe what is included, set availability and keep direct contact details private.

Other Service categories

Transport and dispatch jobs have their own dedicated workflow under Dispatching & Transport.

How an Other Service booking works

Choose service → select date/time and units → see the calculated amount → book/pay through Shop&Drop → provider notified → service performed → customer can confirm completion or report a problem → automatic completion after the protection period if no qualifying dispute is opened → provider payout eligible → provider paid → feedback.

Open-ended quotation-heavy building/painting projects are outside the initial service scope.
