Seller — Confirm Courier Collection

IMPORTANT: ASK YOUR COURIER FOR THE SIX-DIGIT SHOP&DROP COLLECTION PIN AT PICKUP.

Only enter the PIN after the courier has physically collected your sold item. Never confirm collection before handover. Keep your collection receipt.

Authenticated courier tracking or scanning may alternatively confirm pickup. The buyer's delivery confirmation is separate.

My Service Completion PIN

For a paid Other Services booking, keep this six-digit PIN until the work is fully completed. You may share it with your authorized recipient, who can give it to the service provider after completion. Never share it at arrival.

Only share this PIN after the agreed service has been fully completed.

My Dispatch Completion PIN

For a paid dispatch booking, keep this six-digit PIN until the entire delivery job is complete. You may share it with your authorized receiver at the delivery destination. Only give it to the assigned transport provider after completed delivery.

Providers — Confirm Completed Work

For Other Services and Dispatching: after completing your paid job, obtain the six-digit Completion PIN from the customer or their authorized receiver.

The provider must enter that PIN securely into Shop&Drop. One valid completion PIN is sufficient for the agreed completion milestone, subject to payment and dispute safeguards.
