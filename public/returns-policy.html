Returns & Refund Policy

Shop&Drop aims to provide a fair, transparent process for customers, sellers and service providers when a transaction does not go as expected.

This policy explains how customers can report non-delivery, damaged goods, incorrect items, misleading descriptions and other eligible transaction problems.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft. Subject to final administrator, legal and payment-provider review.

Important: Nothing in this policy removes or limits mandatory rights and remedies available under applicable South African consumer law.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to eligible transactions arranged through Shop&Drop, including new, handmade, second-hand and refurbished products, and applicable services.

Transport and courier-related complaints may also be reviewed under the relevant booking, carriage and consumer protection requirements.

The applicable rights and remedies may depend on the nature of the transaction, the seller or provider, the reason for the complaint and the law governing it.

2. Products Not Received

If a customer pays for a product and the product is not delivered, the customer may report the problem through Shop&Drop's dispute process.

Shop&Drop will review the transaction reference, payment confirmation, courier records and available delivery evidence.

Where non-delivery is verified and a refund is due, the customer will receive the applicable item amount through the authorised payment-provider refund process, subject to mandatory law.

A missing or incomplete delivery record must not automatically be treated as proof that a customer received an item.

3. Damaged, Incorrect or Misrepresented Items

Customers may report products that arrive damaged, are materially different from the advertisement, are incorrect or have significant undisclosed defects.

Relevant evidence may include photographs, the listing description, order records, courier information and communications made through the marketplace.

The appropriate remedy may include repair, replacement, return, refund or another remedy required or permitted under applicable law.

Shop&Drop will assess the facts and the legal responsibilities of the relevant seller, supplier or provider.

4. Second-Hand and Pre-Owned Products

Second-hand and pre-owned products must be accurately described, including their condition, known defects and relevant limitations.

Normal wear that was accurately disclosed may be considered when assessing a complaint.

However, describing an item as second-hand, used or sold as-is does not automatically remove consumer rights that apply under South African law.

The legal status of the seller and the particular transaction may affect which statutory protections apply.

5. Change-of-Mind Returns

A general change of mind does not automatically entitle every customer to a refund in every transaction.

However, cancellation, cooling-off and return rights may apply under South African consumer and electronic transaction laws, depending on the transaction and any applicable exceptions.

Shop&Drop will honour mandatory cancellation and return rights where they apply.

Any additional voluntary return arrangement must be clearly agreed and must not reduce statutory rights.

6. Services and Service Bookings

Customers may report services that were not provided, were materially different from the agreed description or were performed improperly.

Relevant booking information, service terms, completion records and customer evidence may be reviewed.

Refund or other remedies will depend on the facts, the applicable law and the service agreement.

Customers and providers must not falsely confirm service completion or misuse a service arrival PIN.

7. Courier and Transport Charges

Courier charges are intended to be allocated to the authorised courier provider through the payment arrangement, rather than being received by Shop&Drop as seller proceeds.

Where an item is not delivered, the applicable product refund and courier-related refund may be handled under separate provider processes.

The responsible courier or transport provider may need to assess a claim under its applicable service terms.

Any statutory entitlement to a refund, including delivery charges where legally required, takes precedence over a conflicting contractual term.

8. Shop&Drop Marketplace Fees

Shop&Drop's buyer-paid marketplace service fee is disclosed before checkout.

Where a transaction is refunded, the treatment of the marketplace fee will depend on the reason for the refund, the service provided, applicable payment-provider charges and mandatory consumer law.

Shop&Drop administration may authorise a fee refund where appropriate.

No marketplace fee or provider charge will be withheld where doing so would conflict with a mandatory legal refund entitlement.

9. Reporting a Problem

Customers should report transaction problems as soon as reasonably possible after discovering them.

Where available, the customer should open the relevant order under My Account and select the appropriate dispute or return option.

Useful information includes:

  • Shop&Drop order reference.
  • Product or service listing reference.
  • Description of the problem.
  • Relevant photographs or evidence.
  • Delivery or service completion details.

Failure to report immediately does not remove statutory rights that remain available under applicable law.

10. Dispute Review and Resolution

Shop&Drop uses transaction records, available evidence and applicable rules to assess reported problems.

Routine eligible refunds are intended to be processed automatically through the authorised payment-provider system when the required conditions are met.

Exceptional or disputed cases may require review by authorised Shop&Drop administrators.

Customers and sellers may be asked for additional information where reasonably necessary.

11. Seller Payout Protection

Seller proceeds are intended to remain subject to authorised payment-provider settlement controls until the applicable verified delivery or completion requirements have been met.

A qualifying unresolved dispute may prevent payout where legally and operationally permitted.

Shop&Drop must not initiate duplicate payouts or refunds for the same transaction.

12. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed through the relevant authorised payment provider, subject to its operational procedures and applicable legal requirements.

The time taken for funds to appear may depend on the payment method, banking system and provider.

Shop&Drop will not promise a specific refund completion time unless it has been verified with the relevant provider.

Customers will be informed of material refund decisions and status changes through supported communication channels.

13. Fraud and Misuse

Fraudulent refund requests, false delivery claims, altered evidence and misuse of confirmation PINs are prohibited.

Shop&Drop may investigate suspected abuse and take lawful, proportionate action to protect customers, sellers and providers.

Legitimate complaints will not be rejected merely because they require investigation.

14. Consumer Rights

Applicable South African legislation may include the Consumer Protection Act, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act and other relevant laws.

The applicability of particular provisions depends on the transaction, the parties and any statutory exceptions.

Nothing in this policy excludes rights that cannot lawfully be waived or restricted.

15. Policy Updates

Shop&Drop may update this policy as payment-provider, courier and marketplace arrangements are finalised.

The final approved policy and effective date will be published before live transactions begin.
