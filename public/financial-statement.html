Recorded Financial Transactions

Payments, allocations, refunds, payout records and release requests are separate records of the same transaction. Do not add these rows or columns to calculate revenue or settlement. Pending and failed amounts are recorded amounts, not money received. A dash means no applicable or supplied amount. Processing and bank charges are not available from this reporting function. Currency is not assumed for records without a stored currency.

Reconciliation Information

Payment handling: Shop&Drop uses provider-held marketplace allocations. The seller's proceeds are not paid out from Shop&Drop's own bank account.

Seller payout: The normal process is automatic: physical-product seller release requires independently verified authorised courier collection; service and transport provider release requires verified customer Completion PIN after the agreed work is complete. This page does not authorize or initiate a release.

Commission: The existing buyer-paid commission rules remain unchanged. This page does not recalculate or modify them.

Final bank reconciliation requires the selected payment provider's authoritative settlement and allocation records.
