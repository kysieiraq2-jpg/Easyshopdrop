Reconciliation Information

Payment handling: Shop&Drop uses provider-held marketplace allocations. The seller's proceeds are not paid out from Shop&Drop's own bank account.

Seller payout: Payout eligibility requires the applicable verified fulfilment or delivery confirmation.

Commission: The existing buyer-paid commission rules remain unchanged. This page does not recalculate or modify them.

Final bank reconciliation requires the selected payment provider's authoritative settlement and allocation records.
