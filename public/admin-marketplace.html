Commission Rules

Configurable product/service commission tiers. Final rates are set after unit-economics testing, not hard-coded permanently.

Returns & Disputes

Transaction-linked cases, evidence, return tracking and payout holds until qualifying disputes are resolved.

Exception Queue

Routine transactions are automated. Admin attention is reserved for risk flags, failed payouts, unusual disputes and provider failures.

Privacy & transaction rule

Provider/seller direct contact and banking details remain private. Shop&Drop mediates the transaction, tracks fulfilment and applies the configured commission before provider payout. Shop&Drop owns no seller inventory and assumes no warehouse or routine office fulfilment staff.
