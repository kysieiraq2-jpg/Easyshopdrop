Prohibited & Restricted Listings

Shop&Drop is an online marketplace connecting customers with independent sellers, service providers and dispatch or transport providers.

These rules explain which products, services and advertisements are prohibited or may require additional review before being accepted.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft, subject to final administrator and legal review.

Important: Shop&Drop does not own, manufacture or directly sell independent advertised products. Sellers and providers remain responsible for the legality and accuracy of their own listings, subject to applicable law.

1. General Listing Requirements

Every listing must comply with applicable South African law and Shop&Drop's marketplace rules.

  • The seller must own the item or be authorised to sell it.
  • The product or service must be lawful.
  • Descriptions and photographs must be accurate.
  • Known defects and relevant restrictions must be disclosed.
  • Listings must not mislead customers about safety, origin or authenticity.
  • Any legally required licences or approvals must be held by the provider.

2. Stolen and Unlawfully Obtained Goods

Stolen, unlawfully obtained or fraudulently acquired products are strictly prohibited.

Sellers must not advertise goods they do not own or lack authorisation to sell.

Shop&Drop may request reasonable proof of ownership where concerns arise.

3. Counterfeit and Infringing Products

Counterfeit goods, fake branded products and items that unlawfully infringe intellectual property rights are prohibited.

Sellers must not falsely represent products as genuine, licensed or manufacturer-approved.

Lawful second-hand branded goods may be advertised when their authenticity and condition are accurately represented.

4. Weapons and Explosives

Shop&Drop prohibits listings for firearms, ammunition, explosives and other weapons or weapon components that the platform does not expressly authorise.

This includes dangerous or restricted weapons and items intended primarily for unlawful violent use.

Ordinary household tools and lawful sporting equipment may be assessed according to their nature, purpose and applicable law.

5. Drugs, Medicines and Controlled Substances

Illegal drugs, controlled substances and unlawfully supplied medicines are prohibited.

Prescription medicines and regulated pharmaceutical products may not be advertised unless expressly authorised by Shop&Drop and legally permitted.

Sellers must not make false or unsupported medical claims about products or services.

6. Hazardous Materials

Dangerous chemicals, toxic substances, radioactive materials and other hazardous products may be prohibited or subject to additional restrictions.

Products requiring special storage, transport, licensing or handling may not be advertised without the necessary legal compliance and platform authorisation.

7. Wildlife and Protected Species

Unlawful wildlife products, protected species, prohibited animal products and items obtained through illegal hunting or trafficking are strictly prohibited.

Any animal-related listing must comply with applicable animal welfare and conservation laws.

8. Adult and Exploitative Content

Illegal sexual content, exploitative material, child sexual abuse material and services involving exploitation are strictly prohibited.

Shop&Drop may also restrict adult-oriented products or services that are unsuitable for the marketplace.

9. Fraudulent and Deceptive Listings

Listings intended to deceive, defraud or mislead customers are prohibited.

  • Fake products or nonexistent stock.
  • False descriptions or manipulated evidence.
  • Fraudulent investment or income promises.
  • Misleading guarantees or warranties.
  • Listings designed to collect private information unlawfully.
  • Attempts to redirect customers to fraudulent payment arrangements.

10. Financial and Regulated Services

Financial services, credit products, insurance, investment offers and other regulated activities may not be advertised without applicable authorisation and Shop&Drop approval.

Illegal gambling, fraudulent financial schemes and deceptive investment promotions are prohibited.

11. Services Requiring Qualifications

Certain services may require professional registration, licensing, insurance or specialist qualifications.

Providers must hold the authorisations required for the services they offer.

Shop&Drop may request supporting information or restrict listings where verification is necessary.

The appearance of a listing does not constitute a professional endorsement by Shop&Drop.

12. Transport and Dispatch Restrictions

Dispatch and transport providers must comply with applicable road, vehicle, licensing, insurance and carriage requirements.

Transporting illegal goods or accepting prohibited shipments is not permitted.

Certain products may require specialist transport arrangements or may be excluded from ordinary courier services.

Independent transport providers remain responsible for their own applicable transport obligations.

13. Second-Hand and Homemade Goods

Lawful second-hand products, handmade furniture, crafts and home-produced goods are generally welcome on Shop&Drop.

Sellers must accurately describe the item's condition, materials, safety characteristics and known defects.

Products subject to specific safety or regulatory requirements must comply with those requirements.

14. Personal Information and Privacy

Listings must not publish another person's private contact details, residential address, identity documents or financial information without lawful authority.

Shop&Drop's public seller aliases and internal account references are intended to help protect user privacy.

Private information provided for an authorised transaction must not be misused or disclosed for unrelated purposes.

15. Off-Platform Payment Solicitation

Sellers and providers must not use Shop&Drop listings to solicit unauthorised off-platform payments or bypass the marketplace's transaction safeguards.

Shop&Drop's payment, delivery confirmation and dispute processes are designed to protect participating customers and providers.

Transactions completed outside the authorised marketplace process may not benefit from these protections.

16. Listing Review and Removal

Shop&Drop may review, reject, suspend or remove listings that breach these rules, create material safety or fraud risks, or violate applicable law.

Where appropriate, Shop&Drop may request clarification or supporting documents from a seller or provider.

Review or publication of a listing does not guarantee that the item or service is lawful, safe or suitable for every customer.

17. Reporting Prohibited Listings

Customers and other users may report suspected unlawful, fraudulent or prohibited listings through Shop&Drop's official feedback or administration channels.

Reports should include the relevant listing reference and a clear description of the concern where possible.

Shop&Drop may investigate and take appropriate action consistent with applicable law.

18. Responsibilities and Legal Rights

Independent sellers and providers remain responsible for their own products, services, listings and legal obligations.

Shop&Drop remains responsible for its own applicable duties as a marketplace intermediary.

Nothing in these rules removes statutory consumer rights or excludes liability where doing so would be prohibited by law.

19. Policy Updates

Shop&Drop may update these rules to reflect changes in law, marketplace operations or provider requirements.

The final approved policy and effective date will be published before live marketplace transactions begin.
