Prohibited & Restricted Listings

Shop&Drop connects independent buyers, sellers, service providers and transport providers through an intermediary marketplace.

These rules identify products, services, advertisements and practices that are prohibited or subject to restrictions under applicable South African law and Shop&Drop marketplace rules.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft. Subject to final legal and administrator review.

Important: Shop&Drop does not ordinarily own, manufacture or directly sell independently advertised products. Sellers and providers are responsible for the legality, accuracy and performance of their own offerings. Shop&Drop retains its own applicable intermediary and legal responsibilities.

1. General Listing Requirements

All listings must comply with applicable South African law and Shop&Drop's marketplace rules.

  • Advertise only products owned or lawfully offered for sale.
  • Advertise only lawful products and services.
  • Use accurate descriptions and photographs.
  • Disclose known significant defects and relevant restrictions.
  • Do not mislead customers about condition, safety, origin or authenticity.
  • Declare accurate service or vehicle operating locations and coverage areas.
  • Hold all licences, registrations and approvals legally required for the activity.
  • Provide accurate prices, available quantities and booking conditions.

Ordinary eligible listings are intended to be published through the marketplace's automated verification process.

Exceptional fraud, safety and legal concerns may require administrator investigation.

2. Stolen and Unlawfully Obtained Goods

Stolen, unlawfully obtained or fraudulently acquired products are strictly prohibited.

A seller must own an item or have lawful authorization to sell it.

Shop&Drop may request reasonable proof of ownership where a legitimate concern arises.

False claims of ownership, misleading serial numbers and unauthorized resale are prohibited.

3. Counterfeit and Infringing Products

Counterfeit goods, fake branded products and products unlawfully infringing intellectual property rights are prohibited.

Sellers must not falsely represent goods as genuine, licensed or approved by a manufacturer.

Lawful second-hand branded goods may be advertised when authenticity and condition are accurately represented.

4. Weapons and Explosives

Shop&Drop prohibits listings for firearms, ammunition, explosives and weapons or weapon components that the platform does not expressly authorize.

This includes dangerous or restricted weapons and items primarily intended for unlawful violent use.

Ordinary household tools and lawful sporting equipment may be considered according to their nature, intended use and applicable law.

5. Drugs, Medicines and Controlled Substances

Illegal drugs, controlled substances and unlawfully supplied medicines are prohibited.

Prescription medicines and regulated pharmaceutical products may not be advertised unless expressly permitted by Shop&Drop and applicable South African law.

Sellers and providers must not make false, deceptive or unsupported health or medical claims.

Regulated pest-control chemicals and related services must comply with applicable registration, handling and licensing requirements.

6. Hazardous Materials

Dangerous chemicals, toxic substances, radioactive materials and other hazardous products may be prohibited or subject to restrictions.

Products requiring special storage, carriage, licensing or handling must not be advertised without the required legal compliance and platform authorization.

Couriers and transport providers must not accept prohibited hazardous shipments through ordinary delivery arrangements.

7. Wildlife and Protected Species

Unlawful wildlife products, protected species, prohibited animal products and items obtained through illegal hunting or trafficking are strictly prohibited.

Any animal-related product or service must comply with applicable animal welfare, conservation and transport requirements.

8. Adult and Exploitative Content

Illegal sexual content, exploitative materials, child sexual abuse material and services involving exploitation are prohibited.

Shop&Drop may restrict adult-oriented listings that are unsuitable for the marketplace.

Listings involving coercion, trafficking, abuse or unlawful exploitation are prohibited.

9. Fraudulent and Deceptive Listings

Listings and activities intended to deceive or defraud another marketplace participant are prohibited.

  • Nonexistent products or intentionally false stock information.
  • False descriptions, manipulated listing photographs or fabricated proof of ownership.
  • Misleading guarantees, warranties or product claims.
  • Fraudulent investment or income promises.
  • Attempts to obtain private personal or financial information unlawfully.
  • Attempts to redirect customers to fraudulent payment arrangements.
  • False courier collection or delivery claims.
  • Fabricated tracking references, waybills or courier scans.
  • Misuse of collection, delivery or completion PINs.
  • False service-completion photographs or altered work records.
  • Misleading GPS, destination handover or equipment-working-time evidence.

A seller or provider must not deliberately submit false information to trigger payment eligibility.

Similarly, buyers must not submit knowingly false non-delivery, refund or service complaints.

Verified courier collection proves custody of a parcel, not the correctness of its contents.

10. Financial and Regulated Services

Financial services, credit products, insurance, investment offers and other regulated activities may not be advertised without applicable authorization and Shop&Drop approval.

Illegal gambling, fraudulent financial schemes and misleading investment promotions are prohibited.

Sellers and providers may not falsely claim to be approved payment providers, banks, insurers or financial service representatives.

11. Services Requiring Qualifications

Some services require professional registration, specialist qualifications, licensing or insurance.

Examples may include regulated electrical, medical, pest-control or specialist technical work.

Providers must hold the legal authorizations required for the work they advertise and perform.

Where verification is reasonably necessary, Shop&Drop may request supporting documentation.

Publication of a listing does not automatically mean Shop&Drop endorses, certifies or guarantees the provider's qualifications or workmanship.

Providers must not claim that ordinary arrival at a work location establishes completion of a service.

12. Transport and Dispatch Restrictions

Transport, courier and dispatch providers must comply with applicable road, vehicle, licensing, insurance and carriage laws.

Providers must accurately advertise their base location, vehicle or equipment type, capacity, availability and geographical coverage.

Transporting unlawful goods or accepting prohibited shipments is not permitted.

Certain goods may require specialized transport or may be excluded from ordinary courier services.

A transport provider must not falsely report collection, arrival, destination handover or completion.

GPS information, courier scans and tracking records must relate to the genuine authorized booking.

Independent transport providers remain responsible for their own legal and contractual transport obligations.

13. Second-Hand and Homemade Goods

Lawful second-hand goods, homemade furniture, handmade crafts and home-produced products are generally welcome on Shop&Drop.

Sellers must truthfully describe condition, materials, relevant dimensions, safety characteristics and known defects.

Handmade variations and ordinary disclosed wear do not automatically constitute misrepresentation.

However, an item advertised as used or handmade must not be deliberately misdescribed or falsely represented.

Products subject to specific food, health, product safety or other regulatory requirements must comply with those requirements.

The applicable consumer rights depend on the transaction and law.

14. Personal Information and Privacy

Advertisements must not publish another person's private contact details, residential address, identity documents or financial information without lawful authority.

Shop&Drop uses public seller aliases and internal account references to help protect private identities.

An advertised service area may identify a town or province without publishing a provider's private street address.

Private completion photographs must not unnecessarily expose children, workers, customer homes, documents or other identifiable personal information.

Courier pickup codes, completion PINs, GPS data and private transaction records must not be published in advertisements.

Necessary information may be shared with authorized participants for an approved booking.

Exceptional disclosure of buyer or seller contact information must have a lawful and documented basis.

See the Privacy Policy .

15. Off-Platform Payment Solicitation

Sellers and providers must not use Shop&Drop listings to solicit unauthorized off-platform payments or evade the marketplace's applicable transaction fees.

They must not attempt to bypass authorized payment verification, courier collection, service completion or fraud-prevention controls.

Shop&Drop's intended payment model uses provider-managed allocations and verified payout milestones.

For physical goods, the intended seller payout milestone is independently verified authorized courier collection.

For Other Services and Dispatching, the intended payout milestone is verified completion of the agreed service or paid working period.

Transactions completed outside the authorized Shop&Drop process may not benefit from its normal automated transaction services.

These revised payout arrangements remain inactive until approved providers and backend verification are ready.

16. Listing Review and Removal

Shop&Drop is designed to publish ordinary eligible listings through automated verification rather than manual approval of every advertisement.

Shop&Drop may review, restrict, suspend or remove listings that breach these rules, present material fraud or safety risks, or violate applicable law.

Where appropriate, Shop&Drop may request clarification or supporting documents from the seller or provider.

The two administrators primarily investigate exceptional matters, complaints and disputes.

Publication or review does not guarantee that an independent product or service is lawful, safe or suitable for every customer.

17. Reporting Prohibited Listings

Customers and other participants may report suspected unlawful, fraudulent or prohibited advertisements through Shop&Drop's authorized feedback channels.

Reports should include the relevant listing reference, the concern and supporting information where reasonably available.

Reports of false courier collection or fabricated service-completion evidence should identify the relevant order or booking reference.

Shop&Drop may investigate and take appropriate proportionate action consistent with law.

Genuine complaints must not be rejected merely because the seller or provider has already received payment.

18. Responsibilities and Legal Rights

Independent sellers remain responsible for their products, advertisements and applicable legal duties.

Independent service and transport providers remain responsible for their services, equipment, contractual performance and legal obligations.

Shop&Drop facilitates transactions and remains responsible for its own applicable duties as a marketplace intermediary.

Shop&Drop does not ordinarily become the seller or manufacturer of independent goods by publishing an advertisement.

Nothing in these rules removes consumer rights, privacy protections or legal obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Failure to read a policy does not automatically waive mandatory statutory rights.

19. Policy Updates

Shop&Drop may revise these restrictions as laws, platform operations, payment providers and courier arrangements develop.

The final approved policy and effective date will be published before live marketplace transactions begin.

The published rules must remain consistent with the actual website and backend functionality.

Material changes will be communicated where required by law.
