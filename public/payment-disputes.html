Chargeback Handling Requirements

The protected dispute workflow must support:

  • Evidence required
  • Accept dispute
  • Contest dispute
  • Provider decision recording
  • Applicable payout holds
  • Authorised refund and ledger reconciliation

These actions require verified payment-provider integration, protected backend authorisation and complete administrator audit records. They are not enabled on this page.
