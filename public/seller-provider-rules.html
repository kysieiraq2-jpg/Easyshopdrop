Seller & Service Provider Rules

Shop&Drop connects customers with independent sellers, service providers and dispatch or transport providers.

These rules explain the responsibilities of people and businesses advertising products or offering services through the Shop&Drop marketplace.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft. Subject to final administrator and legal review.

Important: Shop&Drop operates as a marketplace intermediary. Sellers and independent providers remain responsible for their own products, services, representations and legal obligations, subject to applicable law.

1. Registration and Eligibility

Registration and advertising on Shop&Drop are free.

Eligible individuals, businesses, home-based producers and independent providers may apply to use the marketplace.

  • Provide accurate registration information.
  • Use an account you are authorised to operate.
  • Keep relevant contact information current.
  • Comply with applicable South African law.
  • Provide required licences or authorisations for regulated activities.

Shop&Drop may verify or review accounts where necessary to prevent fraud, protect users or meet legal requirements.

2. Independent Seller and Provider Status

Sellers and providers operate independently unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

Listing an item or service on Shop&Drop does not make the seller or provider an employee, agent or partner of Shop&Drop.

Shop&Drop does not automatically become the owner, manufacturer, supplier or service performer of independent listings.

Each party remains responsible for its own legal obligations, including applicable consumer protection, tax, licensing and safety requirements.

3. Accurate Product Advertisements

Sellers must provide honest and reasonably complete information about the products they advertise.

  • Describe the actual item being offered.
  • Disclose known damage, defects or limitations.
  • Identify products accurately as new, handmade, used or refurbished.
  • Use photographs that fairly represent the item.
  • Advertise only items the seller owns or is authorised to sell.
  • Keep prices and availability accurate.

Misleading descriptions, concealed material defects and false claims are prohibited.

4. Second-Hand and Handmade Products

Shop&Drop welcomes lawful second-hand products, handmade furniture, crafts and goods produced by individuals or businesses.

Sellers must accurately describe the condition and relevant characteristics of each item.

The fact that a product is second-hand or handmade does not automatically remove consumer rights that apply under South African law.

5. Service Provider Responsibilities

Independent service providers must describe their services truthfully, including the type of work, pricing basis, availability and relevant conditions.

Providers are responsible for performing agreed services with the care, skill and standards required by law.

Where an activity requires a licence, qualification, registration or other legal authorisation, the provider must hold it.

Providers must not claim that Shop&Drop personally performs or guarantees their independent services unless expressly authorised in writing.

6. Dispatch and Transport Providers

Independent dispatch and transport providers are responsible for accurately describing their transport services, vehicles, capacity, availability and applicable conditions.

Providers must comply with relevant road, vehicle, insurance and licensing laws.

They must handle collection and delivery information responsibly and use it only for authorised purposes.

Responsibility for loss, damage or delay will be assessed according to the facts, applicable law and the relevant transport terms.

7. Prices and Shop&Drop Commission

Sellers and providers enter the amount they wish to receive for their advertised goods or services.

Shop&Drop applies its buyer-paid marketplace fee to the relevant amount.

  • 10% for amounts below R1,000.
  • 7% for amounts of R1,000 or more.

The customer-facing price and applicable charges must be disclosed before checkout.

Registration and ordinary listing remain free.

8. Payment Processing and Seller Proceeds

Customers are intended to pay through an authorised payment provider during checkout.

Shop&Drop's planned payment arrangement allocates the marketplace commission, applicable courier charge and seller or provider proceeds through the authorised provider.

Shop&Drop does not intend to receive the entire customer payment into its own bank account and then distribute the funds manually.

Seller proceeds are intended to remain under the payment provider's settlement controls until the applicable verified delivery or completion requirements have been met.

Final payment arrangements remain subject to bank, provider and legal approval.

9. Orders and Stock Availability

Sellers must not deliberately advertise unavailable products or sell an item they can no longer supply.

For eligible limited-stock products, Shop&Drop uses a planned 30-minute checkout reservation.

A sale is confirmed only when payment is verified through the authorised payment process.

Sellers should prepare an item for fulfilment only after receiving the appropriate confirmed sale notification.

10. Packaging and Handover

Product sellers are responsible for preparing the correct item and packaging it appropriately for the agreed transport method.

Sellers must provide accurate collection details through authorised marketplace processes.

Couriers and transport providers must follow the applicable collection, tracking and handover procedures.

Users must not falsify collection, delivery or service completion records.

11. Delivery and Completion Confirmation

Shop&Drop may use courier tracking records, proof of delivery, customer confirmation PINs and authorised scanner verification.

The relevant verification requirements depend on the type of transaction.

Seller or provider payout eligibility depends on verified fulfilment and the authorised payment provider's settlement rules.

A confirmation PIN must not be requested or used to falsely record delivery or service completion.

12. Returns, Refunds and Disputes

Sellers and providers must cooperate reasonably with legitimate complaints, returns and disputes.

They may be required to provide listing information, transaction records or other relevant evidence.

Refunds and other remedies will be determined according to the circumstances, applicable transaction terms and mandatory law.

Shop&Drop facilitates dispute handling and authorised payment-provider refund processes.

Shop&Drop does not automatically assume the financial obligations of an independent seller or courier merely because the transaction was arranged on its platform.

13. Privacy and Customer Information

Private buyer and seller contact details, addresses and financial information must not be publicly displayed through listings.

Providers may receive necessary information for authorised bookings, collection or delivery.

Personal information must not be copied, sold, misused or disclosed for unrelated purposes.

Providers must comply with applicable privacy and data protection law.

14. Off-Platform Transactions

Sellers and providers must not use Shop&Drop listings, enquiries or transaction processes to solicit unauthorised off-platform payments or evade applicable marketplace charges.

Users must not attempt to bypass authorised payment, delivery verification or fraud-prevention safeguards.

Shop&Drop cannot provide its normal transaction protections for payments made outside the authorised marketplace process.

15. Prohibited and Restricted Listings

Sellers and providers must not advertise unlawful, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous or otherwise prohibited products or services.

Certain categories may require additional verification or may be restricted under Shop&Drop rules.

The separate Restricted Listings Policy provides further details.

16. Compliance and Account Restrictions

Shop&Drop may review, reject, suspend or remove listings or accounts where reasonably necessary to address fraud, misrepresentation, security concerns, unlawful conduct or breaches of these rules.

Any action will be subject to applicable law and relevant procedural requirements.

Users remain responsible for cooperating with lawful investigations and applicable regulatory obligations.

17. Responsibility and Liability

Independent sellers and providers remain responsible for their own goods, services, representations and contractual obligations.

Shop&Drop is responsible for its own applicable duties as a marketplace operator and intermediary.

Shop&Drop does not provide a general guarantee of independent products or services.

Nothing in these rules excludes responsibility or consumer rights where exclusion is prohibited by law.

18. Changes to These Rules

Shop&Drop may update these rules as its marketplace, payment and provider arrangements develop.

The final approved rules and effective date will be published before live marketplace transactions begin.
