Seller & Service Provider Rules

Shop&Drop connects independent buyers with sellers, service providers, dispatch providers and equipment operators.

These rules describe the responsibilities of people and businesses advertising products or providing services through the Shop&Drop marketplace.

South African launch edition — prelaunch draft. Subject to final legal, administrator and payment-provider review.

Important: Shop&Drop is a marketplace intermediary, not ordinarily the owner or direct seller of advertised goods or the performer of independent services. Sellers and providers remain responsible for their own goods, services, statements and legal obligations.

1. Registration and Eligibility

Registration and ordinary advertising through Shop&Drop are free.

Eligible private individuals, home-based producers, craftspeople, businesses and independent service providers may use the marketplace.

  • Provide accurate registration information.
  • Maintain an authorized account.
  • Keep required contact details current.
  • Comply with applicable South African law.
  • Provide required banking and identity verification information to authorized providers.
  • Hold licenses or qualifications required for regulated activities.

Routine eligibility and listing publication are intended to be processed automatically once applicable verification requirements are satisfied.

Shop&Drop's two administrators primarily handle exceptional complaints, security concerns and disputes.

2. Independent Seller and Provider Status

Sellers and providers act independently unless a separate lawful agreement expressly establishes otherwise.

Using Shop&Drop does not ordinarily make a seller or provider an employee, agent or business partner of Shop&Drop.

The product sale is ordinarily between the independent buyer and independent seller.

A service or transport booking ordinarily creates an agreement between the customer and independent provider.

Shop&Drop facilitates the transaction but does not ordinarily own, manufacture, repair or supply the independently advertised product.

Each participant remains responsible for their own contractual, tax, licensing, safety and applicable legal obligations.

3. Accurate Product Advertisements

Sellers must accurately represent the items they advertise.

  • Advertise only goods owned or lawfully offered for sale.
  • Identify the actual product being sold.
  • Use photographs that fairly show the item.
  • Disclose relevant condition, wear, damage and known defects.
  • Provide accurate dimensions, specifications and quantities.
  • Identify whether goods are new, second-hand, handmade or refurbished.
  • Maintain accurate prices and availability.

False photographs, misleading descriptions, concealed significant defects and counterfeit goods are prohibited.

Each listing receives a Shop&Drop reference that must remain associated with the relevant order, collection and transaction records.

Publication does not mean Shop&Drop independently inspected or certified the advertised item.

4. Second-Hand and Handmade Products

Shop&Drop welcomes lawful second-hand goods, homemade furniture, crafts and other goods made or sold by private individuals and small businesses.

These goods may have normal wear, individual characteristics or handmade variations.

Sellers must describe the actual product honestly and disclose known significant defects or limitations.

Buyers should consider the advertised condition and price before purchasing.

Being second-hand, handmade or advertised as-is does not automatically remove rights under applicable South African law.

Independent buyers and sellers are ordinarily responsible for addressing their own product description or condition disputes, subject to their legal obligations.

5. Other Service Provider Responsibilities

Independent providers offering cleaning, pest control, repairs, pet services or other work must describe their services truthfully and clearly.

Advertisements should identify the type of service, pricing unit, availability, base town, service coverage and material booking conditions.

Additional travel costs must be disclosed and agreed before the customer pays.

Providers must perform the agreed work with the care, skill and standards required by applicable law.

The agreed service must be completed before its payment becomes eligible for release, subject to the verification rules below.

Merely arriving at the customer's property does not establish that the service has been completed.

Providers remain responsible for their own employees, assistants, equipment, licenses and applicable employment obligations.

6. Dispatch, Transport and Equipment Providers

Independent dispatch providers must provide accurate information about available vehicles, equipment, capacity, location, pricing and operating coverage.

Providers must comply with applicable road, vehicle, licensing, safety and insurance requirements.

For ordinary transport jobs, completion means fulfilment of the agreed journey and relevant destination handover.

Appropriate GPS, tracking, handover or recipient confirmation may support completion verification.

For hourly machinery such as cranes, bulldozers or other equipment, the completion milestone may be the end of each agreed paid working period.

Further working periods or additional services require appropriate agreement and payment.

The provider remains responsible for its own transport, work performance and applicable legal duties.

7. Prices and Shop&Drop Commission

Sellers and providers specify the amount they wish to receive for their goods or services.

Shop&Drop applies its buyer-paid marketplace fee:

  • 10% for advertised amounts below R1,000.
  • 7% for advertised amounts of R1,000 or more.

Applicable marketplace fees, product or service prices, and separate transport charges must be disclosed before checkout.

Shop&Drop does not charge an ordinary registration or listing fee.

A seller's or provider's agreed price must not be reduced by an undisclosed marketplace commission.

8. Payment Processing and Seller Proceeds

Customers are intended to make one coordinated checkout through an authorized payment-provider arrangement.

The provider-managed allocations may include the seller or service provider's proceeds, Shop&Drop's commission and applicable courier charges.

Shop&Drop does not intend to receive the full buyer payment into its own bank account and distribute the proceeds manually.

The approved payment provider is intended to control the holding and release of seller and provider funds.

The payout milestones are:

  • Physical goods: verified collection by the authorized courier.
  • Other Services: verified completion of the agreed service.
  • Dispatch: verified completion of the transport job or destination handover.
  • Hourly equipment: completion of the agreed paid working period.

A payout becomes eligible only after payment confirmation, the applicable milestone verification and required financial safeguards.

No routine waiting period is intended solely to allow a customer to withhold completion confirmation.

The time at which funds reach a bank account depends on the approved provider and applicable banking rules.

These revised payout arrangements remain inactive until the provider, legal and technical requirements are approved and tested.

9. Orders and Stock Availability

Sellers must not deliberately advertise items they do not have or cannot supply.

Eligible quantity-limited products are subject to Shop&Drop's planned 30-minute checkout reservation.

A sale is confirmed only when payment is verified through the authorized payment process.

Sellers should prepare items for collection only after receiving a genuine confirmed sale notification.

Failed, cancelled and expired reservations are handled through the applicable inventory reconciliation safeguards.

10. Packaging and Courier Collection

The independent product seller must prepare the agreed item for collection and package it appropriately for the selected transport method.

The seller must provide accurate pickup information through authorized Shop&Drop processes.

The planned collection verification process may include:

  • Courier-held Shop&Drop collection PIN: Shop&Drop sends a six-digit code securely to the authorized courier after confirmed payment and booking. The courier gives it to the seller or handover person when physically collecting the goods, and the seller submits it through My Account.
  • A scan performed by the authorized courier.
  • A matching order and shipment reference.
  • The courier's booking or waybill reference.
  • An authenticated courier report confirming physical collection.

SELLERS MUST REQUEST THE SIX-DIGIT SHOP&DROP COLLECTION PIN FROM THE COURIER AT PHYSICAL PICKUP. Submit it securely in My Account only after handing over the parcel. Keep the collection receipt. Verified courier tracking or scanning may alternatively confirm pickup; the pickup PIN is separate from the buyer's delivery confirmation PIN.

The seller's own declaration that a parcel was collected is insufficient by itself to authorize seller payout.

The collection PIN must remain separate from a buyer's delivery PIN.

The courier's collection record establishes custody of a parcel, not independent certification of its contents or condition.

False collection reports, fabricated scans or misuse of confirmation codes are prohibited.

11. Service and Dispatch Completion Evidence

Other Service providers must report completion through an authorized Shop&Drop process.

Completion evidence may include authenticated photographs linked to the correct booking, time information and relevant location records.

A customer-supplied six-digit completion PIN may serve as an alternative or additional confirmation.

Evidence must genuinely relate to the agreed service and must not be copied, fabricated or intentionally misleading.

Photographs do not automatically prove that the work was performed satisfactorily. Appropriate automated checks and exceptional safeguards may apply.

Customers must have a fair opportunity to report genuine non-performance.

A customer who remains silent must not automatically be able to delay an otherwise properly verified provider payment indefinitely.

Dispatch providers may use verified GPS, destination handover and related transport records where applicable.

Hourly equipment providers may use authenticated working-period records to establish the agreed paid completion milestone.

The intended system must prevent duplicate or unauthorized payment releases.

12. Returns, Refunds and Disputes

Sellers and providers must cooperate reasonably with legitimate complaints, refund claims and disputes.

Independent buyers and sellers are ordinarily the parties responsible for resolving disagreements about the goods being sold.

The seller remains responsible for applicable obligations concerning misrepresentation, material defects and the actual item supplied.

Couriers remain subject to the carriage, custody and delivery obligations imposed by their agreements and law.

Courier insurance must not be assumed to cover every parcel, loss or damage claim.

Shop&Drop may facilitate communication, preserve transaction records and investigate exceptional matters through its two administrators.

Refunds and other remedies depend on the facts, the responsible parties, the payment-provider arrangement and law.

Payment already received by a seller or provider does not automatically extinguish another party's mandatory legal rights.

Please also consult the Returns & Refund Policy .

13. Privacy and Customer Information

Private buyer and seller contact details, residential addresses, banking information and identity documents must not be displayed in public advertisements.

Authorized providers may receive necessary booking, collection, delivery or service information for the relevant transaction.

Collection codes, completion photographs, GPS information and transaction records must be handled securely.

In genuine exceptional disputes, administrators may facilitate communication or disclose limited contact details only when lawful and necessary.

Private personal information must not be copied, sold, published or used for unrelated purposes.

Users must comply with the Privacy Policy and applicable South African data protection requirements.

14. Off-Platform Transactions

Sellers and providers must not use Shop&Drop advertisements to solicit unauthorized off-platform payments or bypass marketplace fees.

Users must not circumvent verified payment, collection, completion or fraud-prevention procedures.

Shop&Drop cannot provide its normal automated transaction services for payments arranged outside its authorized process.

Any lawful direct communication authorized to address a dispute does not automatically authorize misuse of private contact information for unrelated solicitation.

15. Prohibited and Restricted Listings

Sellers and providers must not advertise stolen, counterfeit, fraudulent, unlawful or prohibited products or services.

Certain activities, products, transport categories and materials may require additional licenses, verification or legal compliance.

False safety claims, misleading photographs and unlawful business activities are prohibited.

Please review the Restricted Listings Policy before advertising.

16. Compliance and Account Restrictions

Shop&Drop may restrict, suspend or remove listings or accounts where reasonably necessary to address fraud, misrepresentation, security concerns or unlawful conduct.

A false courier scan, fabricated completion record or unauthorized payment instruction may result in investigation and appropriate action.

Shop&Drop intends to automate routine listing and transaction processing rather than require administrator approval for every ordinary transaction.

Exceptional actions remain subject to applicable law and reasonable procedures.

17. Responsibility and Liability

Independent sellers remain responsible for their own goods and representations.

Independent service, dispatch and equipment providers remain responsible for their own services, equipment and applicable legal duties.

Shop&Drop is responsible for its own applicable duties as a marketplace intermediary.

Shop&Drop does not ordinarily guarantee independent products, workmanship or transport performance.

Nothing in these rules excludes statutory rights or responsibility where exclusion is prohibited by applicable law.

The failure to read a policy does not automatically remove a person's mandatory legal protections.

18. Changes to These Rules

Shop&Drop may update these rules to reflect approved payment providers, courier arrangements, technical processes and legal requirements.

The final rules, effective date and official contact details must be published before live marketplace transactions begin.

Material changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.
