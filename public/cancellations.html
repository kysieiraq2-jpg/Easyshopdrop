Cancellations

For products not yet ordered, remove the item from your Shopping Bag. For an existing order, request cancellation before dispatch or collection. After dispatch, use Returns & Disputes if there is a problem.

For Other Services or Transport Services & Equipment Hire, submit a request before the service or hire starts, subject to the applicable policy.

Submitting this form records a request. It does not itself cancel a paid transaction, release funds, or issue a refund.

Sign in is required to submit. If you need to sign in, return to this page afterwards.
