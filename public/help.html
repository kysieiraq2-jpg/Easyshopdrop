How Shop&Drop Works

Buy. Sell. Shop simply.

Buy • Sell • Find Services • Get It Moved

Shop&Drop is an online marketplace designed for everyone, from private individuals and home-based entrepreneurs to professionals and established businesses.

Registration and advertising are FREE.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make buying, selling and booking services a simple, enjoyable, affordable and secure experience.

Shop&Drop acts as an intermediary between buyers, sellers and service providers, reducing the inconvenience of organising payments, courier services and transaction arrangements independently.

Less effort. Less uncertainty. More convenience.

Courier arrangements are incorporated into checkout when required, and payment verification safeguards help protect all parties against fraud.

Whether you are selling a second-hand item, making products from home, purchasing something new, offering a service or hiring transport and equipment, Shop&Drop brings everything together in one convenient place.

Three Simple Steps

1. Advertise for FREE — List your products, services, transport or equipment at your chosen price.

2. Buy or Book with Ease — Find what you need and complete your purchase or booking through Shop&Drop.

3. Complete the Transaction Securely — Seller and service provider payments are released after the applicable collection or completion requirements have been verified.

Applicable marketplace fees are disclosed as part of the transaction.

Your private contact and financial details remain protected.

Shop&Drop is designed to help you save time, reduce unnecessary expenses and enjoy a simpler way of doing business.

Shop products

Browse New Products or Second-Hand & Pre-Owned through Categories & Search. Add items to your bag, choose eligible delivery and pay through Shop&Drop.

Sell a product — FREE

Registration and product listing are free. You choose the amount you want to receive; Shop&Drop shows its marketplace service fee and the published customer price before the listing goes live.

For physical products: Obtain the courier company’s booking, waybill or tracking reference when the courier physically collects the sold item. Sign in to My Account and use Receive My Payment. Keep the collection receipt. The reference alone does not authorise payout; collection must also be independently verified with the courier.

Other Services

Advertise services such as cleaning, gardening, repairs and catering for free. Customers choose a service, agree on the appointment and price, and pay through Shop&Drop.

Six-digit Completion PIN: The paying customer receives a private PIN for the paid booking. Keep it private until the agreed work is fully completed and you are satisfied. Only then give it to the assigned service provider.

The provider obtains the PIN after completing the work and enters it through My Account — Receive My Payment. Verified completion is required before held proceeds can be released, subject to payment-provider activation and applicable safeguards.

Transport Services & Equipment Hire

Find or advertise transport services, vehicle hire, machinery, equipment and tool hire. Customers choose a provider, agree on the task or hire arrangements and price, and pay through Shop&Drop.

Six-digit Completion PIN: The paying customer receives a private PIN for the paid booking. Only after the entire agreed transport task or equipment hire is completed and the customer is satisfied may the customer or their authorised receiver give the PIN to the assigned provider.

The provider obtains the PIN after completion and enters it through My Account — Receive My Payment. Verified completion, not arrival or collection alone, is required before held proceeds can be released, subject to payment-provider activation and applicable safeguards.

Orders, references and tracking

Your Shop&Drop Order Number identifies the overall buyer order. Each seller/provider portion and each parcel also has its own internal reference for accurate payment, fulfilment and tracking. Technical SD Order and ST reference codes remain available to Shop&Drop internally, while customers see plain-language labels.

Completion, disputes and payout

For physical products, seller proceeds are intended for direct provider-controlled release after verified courier pickup, subject to payment, dispute and fraud checks. The buyer's separate delivery confirmation remains important for tracking and disputes. For Other Services and Transport Services & Equipment Hire, the customer-held six-digit Completion PIN must be verified after the agreed work or hire is completed, before held provider proceeds can be released, subject to payment-provider activation and applicable safeguards.

Communication and privacy

Shop&Drop is designed for automated in-site, email, WhatsApp Business and SMS notifications as integrations are enabled. Only information necessary for payment, fulfilment and delivery is shared with the relevant provider; card/banking details are not exposed between buyer and seller.

Shop&Drop is the marketplace facilitator. Sellers/providers own their goods/services and fulfil transactions; Shop&Drop coordinates the marketplace transaction and handles exceptions.
