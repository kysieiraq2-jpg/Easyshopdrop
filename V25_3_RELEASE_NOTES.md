# Shop&Drop V25.3

Consolidated findings from V25.1/V25.2 mobile testing.

- Separates ordinary Services from Dispatching & Transport in navigation and customer flows.
- Adds a dedicated Dispatching & Transport preview/request page with collection, destination, vehicle type, load, timing and special-requirement fields.
- Adds DR dispatch-request and provider-offer schema foundations while preserving shared Shop&Drop fee/payment/notification/dispute/payout architecture.
- Clarifies tracking with explicit SD Order wording and adds courier selection/booking before courier handover.
- Changes completion policy wording so buyer confirmation can accelerate completion, but seller/provider payout is not held indefinitely: after verified delivery, a protection period can expire automatically when no qualifying dispute is open.
- Makes dispute guidance explicitly SD/ST-aware and prepares automatic linking from My Orders.
- Makes buyer notification wording role-specific; seller/provider payout notices belong to the relevant seller/provider account.
- Refines checkout wording to show ST seller portions, courier/delivery charges, applicable taxes and one SD Order total without exposing private seller payout allocation to the buyer.
- Keeps the paid Business Directory/advertising concept out of the launch plan.
